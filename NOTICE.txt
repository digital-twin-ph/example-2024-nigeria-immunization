DTP coverage and zero-dose children in Nigeria
==============================================

Data:     Utazi CE, Megheib M, Olowe ID, Chaudhuri S, Tejedor-Garavito N,
          Tatem AJ. Modelled vaccination coverage and zero-dose estimates
          for Nigeria, 2000-2024, version 1.0. WorldPop, University of
          Southampton. doi:10.5258/SOTON/WP00880

Terms:    WorldPop states that its datasets are available under the Creative
          Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes:  The years 2005 to 2024 were selected, two archives were joined, some columns
          were left out and boundary coordinates were rounded to 6 decimal
          places. See README.md. Values kept are unchanged.

          The source's boundaries were not reviewed separately. Their licence
          is taken from the WorldPop release that carries them.

These data are model-based estimates. Neither WorldPop nor its funders are
associated with this example, and they have not endorsed it.
